# Phase 3.4 — Subject-Locked Temporal Rendering Forensic Audit

## Executive Summary
This document records the forensic audit of subject pixel motion drift, texture swimming, and local deformation during 2.5D view synthesis in Mode A (`modes/mode_2_5d/`).

## Root Cause Analysis: Source of Subject Pixel Drift

### 1. Monocular Depth Noise Propagation
Monocular depth maps generated by Depth Anything V2 contain high-frequency noise and slight depth variations across adjacent pixels on smooth subject surfaces (e.g., face, ornaments, clothing).
When back-projecting 2D pixels to 3D:
$$X = \frac{(u - c_x) \cdot Z(u,v)}{f_x}, \quad Y = \frac{(v - c_y) \cdot Z(u,v)}{f_y}$$
a noisy depth estimate $Z(u,v)$ places adjacent subject pixels at varying 3D depths $Z_1 \neq Z_2$.

### 2. Independent Per-Pixel Projection Warping
During camera translation $t = (t_x, t_y, t_z)$ and rotation $R$, the 3D position is transformed to $P' = R \cdot P + t$. The projected screen coordinates are:
$$u' = f_x \frac{X'}{Z'} + c_x, \quad v' = f_y \frac{Y'}{Z'} + c_y$$
Because screen displacement $(u' - u)$ varies inversely with depth $Z$, unregularized per-pixel depth variations cause neighboring pixels within the primary subject to displace at different rates, producing visible texture swimming, warping, and non-rigid breathing artifacts.

### 3. Disocclusion Inpainting Flicker
Per-frame Telea inpainting on missing background regions produces inconsistent texture patterns across keyframes ($f_0, f_1, \dots$), creating high-frequency temporal flicker in disoccluded areas.

## Forensic Inventory

- **Current Subject Masks**: Binary mask derived from SAM 2 (`subject_mask.png`), compound grouping, and edge-snapped boundary refinement.
- **Current Depth Data**: Continuous normalized scene depth `depth_map` and background depth plate `bg_depth`.
- **Current Layer Representation**: `LayeredScene` with `BACKGROUND` and `PRIMARY_SUBJECT` `SceneLayer` instances.
- **Current Camera Motion**: Smooth C1 trajectory translations $(t_x, t_y, t_z)$ and rotations $(r_x, r_y, r_z)$ with closed-loop disparity ceiling planning.
- **Current Splatting**: Bilinear subpixel Z-buffered forward splatting with depth discontinuity checks in `geometry/splatting.py`.

## Proposed Subject Lock Architecture

1. **`SubjectAnchor` Representation**: Data structure encapsulating mask, soft mask, bounding box, centroid, reference depth, scale, orientation, and confidence score.
2. **Subject Depth Regularization**: Edge-aware joint bilateral filtering and local noise suppression within subject boundary to create a smooth, coherent depth field while preserving structural 3D gradients.
3. **Subject SE(3) Transform**: Separate global rigid transformation $T_{\text{subject}}(t)$ for primary subject to ensure internal texture stability.
4. **Persistent Background Canvas**: Cross-frame background plate caching to eliminate disocclusion inpainting flicker.
5. **Temporal Subject Reprojection**: Confidence-weighted frame-to-frame reprojection fusion for zero-wobble temporal continuity.
